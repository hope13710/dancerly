We grew up dancing like this.

“Fix the second chorus.” “Wait, which part?”
The video sat in someone’s camera roll. The notes sank in the group chat. By the next rehearsal, nobody remembered which eight.

We practiced. We got better.
But we could never see it.

So we built dancerly: notes that live on the exact count, and every fix, take after take.

Because every dancer deserves to see their progress.
