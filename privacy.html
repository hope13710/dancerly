Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026 · Version 1.0

The short version

  • We collect what we need to run Looproom: your account details and what you and your team put in it — rehearsal videos, notes, and the like.
  • Your videos are visible only to the members of the project they’re in (and to anyone you send a share link to).
  • We don’t sell your data, show ads, or use third-party trackers. We don’t use facial recognition, and we don’t train AI on your videos.
  • Your data is stored in Seoul, South Korea. Some service providers that help us run Looproom are in the United States.
  • You can see, fix, download, or delete your data. Email us and we’ll help.

1. Who we are

Looproom is operated by the operator of Looproom (“we”, “us”), who is the controller of the personal information described here and the person responsible for privacy (Chief Privacy Officer under Korean law). You can reach us at our contact address. This policy applies to the Looproom website, app, and related services.

Where Looproom is used by a team, school, or studio, the project owner decides who is in the project and what is uploaded. For questions about a specific team’s use, you can also contact that project’s owner.

4. Who can see your content

  • Project members can see the videos and team notes in projects they belong to.
  • Private notes are visible only to the person who wrote them and the person they’re for.
  • Share links: anyone with a link you create can watch that one video and its team notes (never private notes), without an account, until you turn the link off.
  • Calendar feed links: anyone with your feed link can see the events it includes.
  • AI summaries you create are yours unless you share them with the project.
  • We access your content only to run and support the Service, keep it secure, or comply with law — for example, when you ask for help or when we must investigate abuse.

6. What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell personal information, and we don’t “share” it for cross-context behavioral advertising (as those terms are used in California law).
  • We don’t show ads or use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.
  • We don’t train AI models on your videos or let our AI provider do so.
  • We don’t make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account details: for as long as you have an account.
  • Videos and team content: until you delete them, or the project owner deletes the project. Content you added to a project stays there after you leave, unless you delete it before leaving.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your profile and the content you created — your videos, notes, replies, and other items, including in projects you shared with others — within 30 days. Projects you created are deleted with everything in them, unless you ask us to move them to another member first. Copies may remain in backups for up to a further 30 days before they’re overwritten.
  • Logs are kept for a limited period for security. Where a law requires us to keep certain records for longer (for example, access logs for 3 months under Korea’s Protection of Communications Secrets Act), we keep only those records, only for that period.

When data is deleted, electronic files are erased so they can’t be restored, and any printed records are shredded.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • access your personal information and get a copy, including in a portable format;
  • correct information that’s wrong;
  • delete your information or your account;
  • restrict or object to certain processing, and withdraw consent where we rely on it;
  • not be discriminated against for exercising your rights; and
  • complain to a data protection authority (see section 13).

You can edit your name and photo, and delete your videos and notes, directly in the app. For anything else, email us from the address on your account. A parent, guardian, or authorized agent may also make a request for you, with proof of authority. We may need to verify your identity. We respond within 10 days, and in any case within the time your local law allows; if we can’t meet a request, we’ll explain why.

9. Children and minors

Looproom isn’t for children under 14, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn that a child under 14 has created an account, we’ll delete it. If you believe this has happened, please contact us.

Dance teams often include teenagers, and rehearsal videos may show minors. Project owners and uploaders are responsible for getting any consent required from minors and their parents or guardians before uploading videos of them (see our Terms). A parent or guardian can ask us to remove a video showing their child.

10. Security

  • All connections are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Passwords are stored only as secure hashes.
  • Access rules in our database ensure people can only see projects they belong to. Videos are kept in private storage and played through links that expire.
  • Access to systems is limited to what’s needed to run the Service, and our providers keep their data centers physically secured.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we’ll notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

11. Cookies and local storage

We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies. We use your browser’s local storage only for things the Service needs or you choose: keeping you signed in, your language, and display preferences (such as mirror mode or a hidden checklist). You can clear it in your browser settings; you’ll be signed out. Our home page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

12. Changes to this policy

We’ll post any update here with its effective date and tell you in the app or by email at least 7 days before it takes effect — or at least 30 days before, if the change affects your rights significantly. Where the law requires your consent for a change, we’ll ask for it.

1. 개인정보처리자

Looproom은 Looproom 운영자(이하 “운영자”)가 운영하며, 운영자가 이 방침에서 설명하는 개인정보의 처리자이자 개인정보 보호책임자입니다. 연락처: 문의 이메일. 이 방침은 Looproom 웹사이트, 앱과 관련 서비스에 적용됩니다.

팀, 학교, 학원 등이 Looproom을 이용하는 경우 프로젝트 관리자가 멤버 구성과 올릴 영상을 정합니다. 특정 팀의 이용에 관한 문의는 해당 프로젝트 관리자에게도 할 수 있습니다.

4. 콘텐츠를 볼 수 있는 사람

  • 프로젝트 멤버는 자신이 속한 프로젝트의 영상과 팀 공개 피드백을 볼 수 있습니다.
  • 비공개 피드백은 작성자와 받는 사람만 볼 수 있습니다.
  • 공유 링크: 회원이 만든 링크가 있는 사람은 링크를 끌 때까지 계정 없이 그 영상 하나와 팀 공개 피드백을 볼 수 있습니다(비공개 피드백은 제외).
  • 캘린더 구독 링크: 링크가 있는 사람은 포함된 일정을 볼 수 있습니다.
  • AI 요약은 프로젝트에 공유하지 않는 한 만든 회원만 볼 수 있습니다.
  • 운영자는 서비스 운영·지원·보안 유지 또는 법령 준수를 위해서만 콘텐츠에 접근합니다(예: 회원이 도움을 요청하거나 부정 이용을 조사해야 하는 경우).

  • 영상과 팀 콘텐츠: 회원이 삭제하거나 프로젝트 관리자가 프로젝트를 삭제할 때까지 보유합니다. 프로젝트를 나가도 회원이 올린 콘텐츠는 프로젝트에 남으며, 나가기 전에 직접 삭제할 수 있습니다.
  • 탈퇴하면 30일 이내에 프로필과 회원이 만든 콘텐츠(다른 사람과 함께 쓰던 프로젝트에 올린 영상, 피드백, 답글 등 포함)를 삭제합니다. 회원이 만든 프로젝트는 그 안의 모든 콘텐츠와 함께 삭제되며, 탈퇴 전에 요청하면 다른 멤버에게 옮겨 드립니다. 백업에 남은 사본은 최대 30일 안에 덮어써져 삭제됩니다.
  • 접속 기록은 보안을 위해 제한된 기간만 보관합니다. 법령에서 보관을 요구하는 경우(예: 통신비밀보호법에 따른 로그 기록 3개월) 해당 정보만 그 기간 동안 보관합니다.

파기 방법: 전자 파일은 복원할 수 없는 방법으로 삭제하고, 종이 기록은 분쇄하거나 소각합니다.

9. 아동과 미성년자

Looproom은 만 14세 미만 아동을 대상으로 하지 않으며, 만 14세 미만 아동의 개인정보를 알면서 수집하지 않습니다. 만 14세 미만이 가입한 사실을 알게 되면 해당 계정을 삭제합니다. 그런 경우를 알고 계시면 연락해 주세요.

댄스팀에는 청소년이 많고, 연습 영상에 미성년자가 나올 수 있습니다. 미성년자가 나오는 영상을 올리기 전에 본인과 법정대리인에게 필요한 동의를 받을 책임은 프로젝트 관리자와 영상을 올린 회원에게 있습니다(이용약관 참고). 법정대리인은 자녀가 나오는 영상의 삭제를 요청할 수 있습니다.
