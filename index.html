Questions, answered

Good to know
before you start.

Who is Looproom for?+

Looproom is for online dance coaches, choreographers, performance team leaders, and small studios that review practice videos remotely.

Can dancers use it on mobile?+

Yes. Dancers can open feedback, jump to a timestamp, upload from their camera roll, and submit a new version from a phone or tablet.

Are videos private?+

Yes. Videos are private to your workspace by default. There are no public profiles or searchable videos unless you intentionally add a future sharing feature.

How does my team get started?+

Create a workspace, make your first Review, and copy the invite link. Dancers can join without a sales call or technical setup.

Can I try it before paying?+

Yes. Start with a free workspace and experience the core feedback loop before choosing a paid plan.
