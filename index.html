Questions, answered

Good to know
before you start.

Why not just use group chat and a shared drive?+

They’re great for talking, not for rehearsing. A note in chat isn’t attached to the moment, the next take lands in a new thread, and nothing tells you which corrections actually landed. Looproom keeps the video, the note, and the next version together.

Does the AI give feedback on our dancing?+

No. AI reports only summarize notes your teammates and coaches actually wrote, and every line links back to its source note. Members can generate their own recap anytime; recaps a leader or coach sends are reviewed by them first. The feedback stays human.

What does the dashboard measure?+

By default, practice and feedback activity: submissions, section loops, notes by category, and how many takes a correction took to fix. Motion signals such as timing against the beat are an opt-in beta, calculated from the video and only compared with a dancer’s own previous takes.

Who is Looproom for?+

Dance teams and crews, university and school clubs, cover groups, and the coaches, choreographers, and studios who work with them. You don’t need a coach to use it — any team can give each other feedback.

Do we need a coach to use it?+

No. Anyone invited to a project can watch the videos, pin notes, sketch, and @mention teammates. If you do work with a coach, they join as part of the team and their notes are marked “Coach”.

How do we keep peer feedback kind and useful?+

Notes are pinned to a moment, not to a person, which keeps them about the dancing. Teammates can 👍 a note they agree with instead of repeating it, and leaders can resolve or hide notes. AI recaps highlight what several teammates noticed, so the most useful feedback rises to the top.

Does my team need to install anything?+

No. Teammates open your invite link in their phone’s browser and join with just their name — no sign-up form, no app. They can watch your notes and sketches, jump to the timestamp, loop the section in mirror mode, reply, and upload their next take from the camera roll.

What videos can I upload?+

Regular phone videos work best — MP4, MOV, or WebM, straight from the camera roll. No editing or exporting needed.

Who can see our videos? Can we delete them?+

Only people you invite to your workspace. There are no public profiles and nothing is searchable. You can delete any video or review at any time, and export everything whenever you want. We don’t use your videos or notes to train AI models unless you explicitly opt in.

How does billing work outside the US?+

Plans are priced in USD and billed monthly by card. Your bank handles currency conversion. You can start on the free plan without a card.
