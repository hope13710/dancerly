Questions, answered

Good to know
before you start.

Why not just use group chat and a shared drive?+

They’re great for talking, not for coaching. A note in chat isn’t attached to the moment, the next take lands in a new thread, and nothing tells you which corrections actually landed. Looproom keeps the video, the note, and the next version together.

Who is Looproom for?+

Online dance coaches, choreographers, performance team leaders, and small studios that review practice videos between sessions.

Do my dancers need to install anything?+

No. Dancers join from your invite link in their phone’s browser. They can watch your notes, jump to the timestamp, loop the section, reply, and upload their next take from the camera roll.

What videos can I upload?+

Regular phone videos work best — MP4, MOV, or WebM, straight from the camera roll. No editing or exporting needed.

Who can see our videos? Can we delete them?+

Only people you invite to your workspace. There are no public profiles and nothing is searchable. You can delete any video or review at any time.

How does billing work outside the US?+

Plans are priced in USD and billed monthly by card. Your bank handles currency conversion. You can start on the free plan without a card.
