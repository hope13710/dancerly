Good to know

Is dancerly really free?

Yes. Every feature is free for everyone on your team. Each team owner gets 5 GB of video storage, about 140 practice videos. If we ever add paid plans, we’ll tell you well in advance, and everything you’ve uploaded stays yours.

Do dancers need to download an app?

No. dancerly runs in the browser on any phone, tablet, or laptop. Share an invite link and they’re in.

What about big iPhone videos?

Videos are compressed on your phone before upload, so even long 4K clips go up quickly. If the connection drops, the upload continues where it stopped. Just pick the same file again.

Who can see our videos?

Only people in your project. Private notes are visible to just two people. You can make a read-only guest link for a single video, and it expires automatically.

How do AI recaps work?

A recap summarizes the notes your team already wrote. Every line links to the notes it’s based on, and anything without a source is left out. Private notes never go into a team recap.

Does it work for K-pop dance covers?

Yes. Upload each practice video as a take, pin notes on the counts where you drift from the original, and compare takes side by side until it’s ready to film.

Can coaches give feedback to one student?

Yes. Choose “Only [name]” when you pin a note, and only you and that student can see it and its replies.

What happens if we run out of storage?

Nothing is deleted. New uploads pause until you remove old takes, or email help@dancerly.app and we’ll add more. Videos are compressed on your phone before upload, so 5 GB goes a long way.

Can we delete everything later?

Yes. Owners can delete videos or whole projects at any time, and you can leave a project whenever you like.
